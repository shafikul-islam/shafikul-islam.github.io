A robotic WAAM cell that watches itself

In wire arc additive manufacturing, an industrial robot carries a welding torch and builds a metal part one bead at a time. Each layer reheats the ones below, so heat, bead shape, and porosity drift during the build.

My cell adds the senses a human welder uses: a camera on the melt pool, an acoustic sensor listening to the arc, and electrical and thermal signals. Learned models turn these signals into a process state, predict defect risk, and suggest a bounded correction to heat input or travel speed before the defect is printed.

  • Sense: vision, sound, thermal
  • Predict: defect risk
  • Act: heat and speed

Designing a world robots can work in

Robots fail less when the factory is designed for them. I measure how much the environment matters, model it, and design better cells in a digital twin before building them for real. Then real trials recalibrate the twin (sim to real and back).
